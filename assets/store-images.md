These are promotional compositions based on existing captures, not new checks of the example books. Originals are
preserved. The extension build copies only icons, so these files are not shipped in the extension package.

Input: `Screenshot1.png` (actual extension screenshot).

Prompt:

> book identity accurately; do not invent UI or results or affiliation. Cream background, dark charcoal text, restrained
> muted teal accent. At top left a small label 'STORYGRAPH KINDLE UNLIMITED CHECKER'. Large headline on two lines:
> 'Check Kindle Unlimited' and 'while browsing StoryGraph'. Smaller subtitle: 'See the result on the book page. Confirm
> on Amazon.com.' Below, use an enlarged tightly cropped portion of the supplied real screenshot centered on Verity
> book title, available status panel, Check again and View on Amazon, with some book cover context; remove unrelated
> lower page, social activity and navigation by cropping. Make the real panel and its buttons very readable, the
> screenshot should occupy most width below header. Do not redesign the UI inside screenshot. At bottom a small note:
> 'Checks Amazon.com. Availability can change.' No logos added, no badges, no claims about reviews, safety, speed, free
> or endorsement. The purpose is legibility and faithful demonstration. Output only this one image.
